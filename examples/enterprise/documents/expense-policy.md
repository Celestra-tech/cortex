# Northwind Expense Policy

Version 3.2. Owner: Finance Operations.

## Scope

This policy applies to every Northwind employee and contractor who incurs costs
on behalf of the company.

## Approval limits

- Expenses up to 500 USD need approval from the employee's direct manager.
- Expenses between 500 USD and 5,000 USD also need approval from the department head.
- Expenses above 5,000 USD need approval from the CFO before the purchase is made.

## Travel

Book economy class for flights under six hours and premium economy for longer
flights. Hotels are reimbursed up to 250 USD per night in tier-one cities and
180 USD per night elsewhere. Meals are reimbursed up to 75 USD per day while
travelling.

## Submitting expenses

Submit receipts in the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase. Expenses
submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed. Reimbursements are paid with the
next payroll run after approval.
